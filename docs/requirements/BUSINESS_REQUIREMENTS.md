# Business Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

The Enterprise Lab Project is a practice management platform designed for professional service practices that manage multiple clients and provide services across different business areas.

The project is inspired by the general category of practice management systems but is an independently designed learning and portfolio project.

## 2. Business Problem

Small and medium professional service practices may manage multiple clients and provide services across different areas such as:

- Client relationship management
- Payroll
- Bookkeeping
- Tax and accounts
- Audit
- APCT
- Other practice-specific services

Managing clients, responsibilities, work, deadlines, assignments, and progress across these different areas can become fragmented.

## 3. Proposed Solution

The system will provide a central platform where a professional practice can manage:

- Its practice and employees
- Clients
- Service areas
- Work items
- Employee assignments
- Work status and progress

The system should provide a clear relationship between:

Practice
→ Client
→ Service Area
→ Work Item
→ Assignment and Status

## 4. Core Business Goal

Provide a central platform for a professional practice to manage clients and track work across multiple service areas.

## 5. Initial Project Scope

The initial version of the project will focus on the following core domain:

Practice
→ Clients
→ Service Areas
→ Work Items
→ Assignment
→ Status Tracking

The initial version will not attempt to recreate a complete enterprise practice management platform.
